📊 Predictive Consumer Purchase & Multi-Channel Campaign Analytics

📋 Project Overview

This comprehensive Power BI project analyzes a retail marketing ecosystem across three dimensions: Campaign Performance, Consumer Demographics, and Purchase Drivers. It transforms raw customer data into actionable insights to optimize marketing spend and improve targeting.

🔗 Dashboards at a Glance

Campaign Analytics: Evaluates the ROI of 6 distinct campaigns and identifies Wine and Meat as the primary revenue engines.

Consumer Analytics: Deep dives into the customer profile, revealing that highly educated individuals (Graduation/PhD) form the core customer base.

Purchase Analytics: Uses Power BI’s Key Influencers AI visual to determine that high income and household composition are the strongest predictors of high sales and campaign acceptance.

💡 Top Business Insights

The "Premium" Driver: High income (over ₹67,381) is the #1 influencer for increased sales. Similarly, income over ₹60,585 makes a customer 7.82x more likely to accept Campaign 1.

Product Hierarchy: Wine is the dominant category, generating ₹681K in sales, nearly double the next best category, Meat (₹374K).

The Ideal Customer: The highest volume of purchases comes from customers with no children at home (58%) and those with a Graduation-level education.

Channel Strategy: Customers prefer buying in-store (13.0K) over the web (9.2K), suggesting that physical locations remain the strongest touchpoint despite a digital presence.

🛠️ Technical Skillset 

Advanced AI Visuals: Implemented Key Influencers to automate root-cause analysis for sales fluctuations.

Data Modeling: Managed a dataset tracking 2.24K unique customers across multiple variables like education, marital status, and household size.

Complex Visualizations: Utilized Donut charts for demographic splits, error bars for salary variance across camps, and stacked bar charts for product-to-campaign performance.

Feature Engineering: Calculated metrics such as Average Age (57.19) and Count of Purchase (8.96K) to provide high-level KPIs for stakeholders.

🚀 Impact 

Focus on Wine & Meat: Allocate 60% of the marketing budget to these categories as they drive the bulk of the revenue.

Target High-Income Professionals: Tailor Campaign 1 style messaging specifically to the "Graduation/PhD" segment with incomes above ₹60K to maximize conversion rates.

In-Store Promotions: Since physical stores lead in volume, implement "Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store" to bridge the gap between web visits and store sales.
